Your steps, nothing else

Stampede reads only your step count from Apple Health. Other players see your username and steps for the day, and that’s it. No ads, and we never sell your data. When there aren’t enough players at your rank, we fill your group with pacers: computer-controlled walkers who keep a realistic pace.
