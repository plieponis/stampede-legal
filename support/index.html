Support

Need help with Stampede? Email us at support@stampedesteps.com and we’ll get back to you. If you can, tell us your username and what you were doing when the problem happened.

How Stampede works

Every day, you race 9 other walkers at your rank. The day runs from 11 PM to 11 PM Eastern Time, and whoever has the most steps at the end wins. Finish in the top zone to move up a rank, or in the bottom zone to move down. The ranks, from lowest to highest, are Mouse, Hare, Fox, Stag and Wolf.

My steps aren’t showing up

  • Stampede reads your steps from Apple Health. Check that it’s allowed: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Stampede, and turn on Steps.
  • Your steps are counted from 11 PM Eastern Time, not midnight, so your total can be different from the Health app’s.
  • Steps you type into the Health app by hand don’t count.
  • Open Stampede to send your latest steps. It also tries to send them in the background, but iPhone decides when.

My steps differ from my Apple Watch

Stampede counts steps the way Apple Health does, combining your iPhone and Apple Watch without counting any step twice. Small differences usually mean the Health app hasn’t synced with your watch yet.

When are the results?

The day ends at 11 PM Eastern Time. We wait 15 minutes for late steps to arrive, then settle the results at about 11:15 PM. You’ll see where you finished the next time you open the app.

Notifications

Stampede sends two notifications a day: one at 2 PM Eastern Time with your current place, and one after the day ends with where you finished. To turn them on or off, open Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications, then Stampede.

Who are the other walkers?

When there aren’t enough players at your rank, we fill your group with pacers: computer-controlled walkers who keep a realistic pace, so there’s always a race.

Reporting a username

If a username is offensive, touch and hold it on the leaderboard, then tap Report Username. We’ll review it and remove it if it breaks our rules.

Deleting your account

In Stampede, open Settings and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account, username, step history and results, and disconnects Stampede from your Apple Account. You can also email us and we’ll delete it for you.

Privacy

Stampede reads only your step count from Apple Health, and uses it only to run the competition. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
